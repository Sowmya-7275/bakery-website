Our Story

Passion, tradition and a little bit of magic in every bake

Welcome to Sowmya Lakshmi Bakery

Named after the founder’s mother, Sowmya Lakshmi Bakery began as a small home kitchen experiment and has grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakery known for its warmth and quality.

We believe that the best baked goods come from pure ingredients, patient techniques and a genuine love for the craft. Every morning our ovens start early so that you can enjoy the freshest breads, pastries and cakes.
